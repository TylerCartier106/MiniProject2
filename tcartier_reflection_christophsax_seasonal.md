# Reflection: christophsax_seasonal

**Summary:** Bursty, declining project. Its longest gap (Jan-Nov 2018, 11 months) follows a Dec 2017 release-prep flurry and ends with commits adding the JSS citation and turning the JSS article into a vignette. The same maintainer returned. The reason is inferred, not stated. Status is Active per GitHub (last commit 2026-09-17), although WoC data stops at 2025-02-28.

## Inactivity pattern
Activity pattern: declining and bursty. Commits were heavy from 2013 to 2016 (up to about 90 a month), then dropped to long quiet stretches with occasional spikes. There are 8 gaps of 3+ months; the longest runs 11 months (Jan to Nov 2018).

## Interpreting the longest gap
Interpreting the gap: moderately easy. The last commits before it (Dec 2017) look like release prep ('v no and date', 'list Dirk as ctb'). The first commits after it add the JSS citation and turn the JSS article into a vignette, and the vignette describes itself as a version of the 2018 Journal of Statistical Software article by Sax and Eddelbuettel. So the maintainer's time most likely went into writing that paper. That is an inference, since no message says it.

## Recovery and current status
Recovery: the same maintainer (Christoph Sax) came back, with 289 commits after the gap. Recent commits (2024 to 2025) are bug fixes, tests and NEWS updates, and GitHub shows a commit in Sept 2026, so I marked it Active.
