## Inactivity pattern
Activity pattern: irregular. There was a burst in 2017 to 2018, then small spikes and long quiet stretches. There are 7 gaps of 3+ months; the longest is 15 months (Dec 2022 to Feb 2024).

## Interpreting the longest gap
Interpreting the gap: moderately hard. Before the gap the work is mostly outside contributors removing unbound or unused type parameters, and the maintainers only show up for 'Update Project.toml'. No message gives a reason, so a stable low-level package that nobody actively develops is my guess.

## Recovery and current status
Recovery: yes, by new people. A typo fix by Cameron Pfiffer came first, then Guillaume Dalle's 'Start DI integration' in Dec 2024 (a later CompatHelper commit in the data shows DI means DifferentiationInterface). The project is still active, with a bot and maintainer commits through 2025.
